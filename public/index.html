The Wall

A living, open canvas for the ANU community.

Leave a creative trace, draw, write, drop in a song or a link — see other people doing the same, live. Nobody needs an account or a real name to take part; nobody is ranked, liked or followed here.

Forking makes your own independent copy of this canvas. Nothing you do after forking affects the original, and nothing that happens on the original affects your fork — it's a clean break, a new space to take in your own direction.
