# Playtests

Evidence for the claims that cannot be checked by code, starting with promise 1: **the game rewards reading your opponent, not just the board**. README.md says which claims are judged; this file is how they were judged.

## What to watch for

- Does a new player understand crashes, rubble and the shield within one game without being told?
- Do players talk about guessing each other ("I knew you'd go there")? That is the sign promise 1 holds.
- Does anyone deliberately crash to block a line?
- When is the shield used, and does the opponent react to it still being available?
- Is 15 seconds too short or too long (open decision D2)?
- Can everything be done on a phone without help?

## Format

```
### P<number>. <date>, <who, described without full names if they prefer>
- **Devices:** phone model or laptop, browser
- **Mode:** computer, friend link or quick match
- **What I asked them to do:**
- **What I saw:** behaviour, quotes, where they got stuck
- **What it tells me about promise 1:**
- **What I changed as a result:** link to commits or corrections
```
