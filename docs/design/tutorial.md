# First-time tutorial

Rubble has rules a newcomer has never seen: both players move at once, matching columns crash into rubble, each player has one shield and one Earthquake, rounds are timed and the board shrinks over time. The tutorial teaches all of it, and how to play with others, in eleven short steps.

## Rules (to add to docs/rules.md)

- **R47** The tutorial is shown once, automatically, the first time a person starts a game in their browser (Play the computer, Play a friend, Join with a code or a friend's link), before that game begins (changed 31 October 2026; see docs/rules.md).
- **R48** Finishing or skipping the tutorial is saved with the player, so it does not return after a refresh, a new session, a restart or a redeploy.
- **R49** The tutorial can be skipped at any step and replayed at any time from "How to play".
- **R50** Tutorial games never change the leaderboard.

## Steps (rewritten 29 October 2026, Part 7 of the fix list; replaces U41 to U46)

The tutorial is an overlay over a practice board against a scripted computer. It is interactive wherever an action is involved, but **Next is always available** (changed 2 November 2026, U167): a player can move on without doing a step's action. A progress line (`tutorial-progress`) says **"Step N of 11"**. **Skip tutorial** (`tutorial-skip`) is always there and ends the whole tutorial straight away. There is no disc left in the top hole. **There is no text under the board** (no hint, no opponent status line): the instructions above the board are the only text.

- **U136** **When it shows (R47, changed 31 October 2026).** It opens only when a person is about to play **their first game**, over the screen they started from and before the game: on home when they tap Play the computer, Play a friend or join with a code (after the code popup accepts a code), and on a game link (`/g/CODE`) **only if the seat check says a player seat is free** (R100); the page asks first and waits for the answer. It does **not** open: on home or any other page by itself; for a spectator (a link to a game that is full, which goes straight to watching, `/watch`); for a game that has **ended** (the page goes straight to its result); or for a signed-in player whose account has finished a game or has seen it (R48). Whether it has been seen is kept in the browser's local storage (`rubble-tutorial-seen`) and on the account. **Quick match shows it on `/play/quick` before the player joins the queue (changed 2 November 2026, U171), and a friend's link has the player take their seat first (R102, U169).** The last step's main button reads **"Start the match"** (`tutorial-next`) and starts the game the person chose; Skip starts it too. From How to play the last button reads **"Done"** and Skip reads **"Close"**, and no game starts.

1. **U137 The goal.** "Get four of your discs in a row, across, down or diagonally." The practice board shows four red discs in a row glowing. No action: Next shows at once.
2. **U138 Pick a column.** "Tap a column to choose it. Your choice locks in and cannot be changed this round." The player taps a column; the ghost disc locks in.
3. **U139 You both move at once.** "Your opponent chooses at the same time. Neither of you sees the other's choice, and both discs drop together." The player taps a column; the computer chooses another and both drop.
4. **U140 The round timer.** "Each round lasts up to 10 seconds, and the bar under the board shows the time left. If you do not choose in time, you miss the round and drop nothing. If both players miss 3 rounds in a row, the game is abandoned." A practice timer bar drains; the player taps a column before it runs out (it never runs out on its own).
5. **U141 Crashes.** "If you both pick the same column, the discs crash and leave rubble. Rubble blocks lines for both players." The player taps a column and the computer picks the same one: rubble.
6. **U142 Your shield.** "The computer is about to win in the glowing column. Arm your shield, then pick that column. The shield wins one crash, once per game, and is used up even if there is no crash." The player arms the shield and taps the glowing column; their disc lands and blocks the win.
7. **U143 Earthquake.** "Earthquake uses your turn: the bottom row crumbles and everything drops down one. Lines it makes by itself do not win. It works once per game, and only when the bottom row has a piece." The player uses it to break up the computer's line.
8. **U144 The shrinking board.** "After round 12, and again after round 20, the top row closes. A warning shows the round before. Plan ahead: space runs out." The warning shows, the player taps a column, and the top row turns to rock.
9. **U145 Draws.** "If you both complete a line in the same round, or the board fills up, it is a draw." The board shows both players completing a line at once. No action.
10. **U146 Playing with friends.** *Rewritten 31 October 2026.* "Play a friend gives you a link and a 6-letter code to send to anyone, anywhere in the world. The first person to open it plays you. If you send it to more people, the second person to open it, and anyone after them, can watch the game live as a spectator." A small illustration (`tutorial-friends`, drawn with shapes, no words) shows **you** (an avatar with a red disc), **the first friend joining as your opponent** (an avatar with a yellow disc, joined to you by a line) and **the second friend as a spectator** (an avatar with an eye icon, outside the pair). No action. The illustration sits in the practice board's place and takes no more room than the board (Q15).
11. **U147 Leaving and playing again.** "Leaving a game counts as a forfeit, so you lose. After a game you can play the same person again with Play again. On a keyboard: keys 1 to 7 pick a column, S arms your shield, E uses Earthquake." No action. This is the only place the keys are written down (U18).

- **U47** Every caption is short, uses the words in `docs/glossary.md` and is readable on a 320px screen. Captions are announced in an `aria-live` region. The step's title is its name above ("The goal", "Pick a column", and so on).
- **U48** The tutorial works by tap, mouse and keyboard, and under reduced motion every step still makes sense without animation.

## Fitting one screen (added 31 October 2026, Q15 to Q17)

- *Superseded by U167 on 2 November 2026 (ADR 0016), kept as history.* **U148** *Order replaced by U159 on 31 October 2026.* The tutorial card never scrolls, at 390 x 844, 1366 x 768 and 320 x 568, on every step. The gap between the caption and the practice board is small (8px), the board is sized from the height left over (never below what keeps the columns tappable), and under the board come, with at least 16px between each, the step's action button (Arm shield or Use Earthquake, when the step has one), Next and Skip (on a short or narrow phone, Next and Skip share one row 8px apart, each at least 44px tall). Nothing, including a caption, status line or button, overlaps the board (Q16). If space is short the gaps shrink first, then the board, never a button below 44px tall.

## The tutorial's fixed order (added 31 October 2026, Part K of the second fix list)

- *Superseded by U167 on 2 November 2026 (ADR 0016), kept as history.* **U159** Top to bottom: the **step counter**, the **title**, the **caption**, the **opponent's status on its own centred line**, **16px**, **the ghost disc lane** (one disc's height, the practice board's top margin; there is no separate element), **8px**, the **practice board**, **16px**, the **hint text** (`tutorial-hint`), **16px**, the **step's buttons** (Arm shield or Use Earthquake where the step has one, then Next) **16px apart**, then **Skip**. *Tightened 31 October 2026: the hint sits 16px below the board, never against its edge, and no gap anywhere in this order is larger than 24px.* **No text, card, badge or button ever enters the ghost lane**, with a disc showing or not, so nothing moves when a disc appears. The caption is never overlapped by the ghost disc. If space is short the board shrinks, never the lane or the gaps (Q15 still holds at 320 x 568).

## One fixed-size card (added 2 November 2026, ADR 0016; replaces U148, U159 and every earlier layout rule for the card)

- **U167** *The tutorial card, its three areas and its board are fixed sizes, not sized by content.* Designed for the two marking viewports, phone 390 x 844 and laptop 1366 x 768. At each viewport the card is **one size, centred on screen, identical on all 11 steps**: width `min(560px, 100vw - 32px)`, height `min(720px, 100vh - 32px)`.
  1. **Padding.** Top and bottom padding are equal to each other, and left and right padding are equal to each other, but the two pairs may differ (phone: 10px top and bottom, 16px left and right; laptop: 24px top and bottom, 32px left and right). Every element stays inside that padding.
  2. **Three fixed areas, top to bottom, each the same height on every step.**
     - **Top:** the step counter, the title and the instructions. No instruction is changed, shortened or shrunk. All 11 instructions are measured at each viewport and the area is fixed to the height of the one with the most lines. Content sits at the top of the area; shorter instructions leave blank space below. Measured (the instruction font is 1.05rem on both): **phone 390 x 844: 7 lines (step 10, "Playing with friends"), area 204px; laptop 1366 x 768: 4 lines (steps 4, 7, 10 and 11), area 140px.** (Padding: phone 10px top and bottom, 16px left and right; laptop 24px top and bottom, 32px left and right.) The area also holds the step counter and the title; the heights above include them.
     - **Middle:** the board, centred horizontally and vertically in this area. Its size is set once per viewport (phone 316px wide, laptop 310px wide) and never changes between steps or for any other reason. Everything a step needs around the board has space reserved inside this area, so it never moves the board: the ghost disc's lane above it, and below it one 44px slot shared by the timer bar, the warning banner and the power-up buttons (a step shows at most one of them). The friends picture (step 10) sits in the board's place and the board keeps its place, hidden.
     - **Bottom:** **Next**, centred, with **Skip tutorial** centred below it, in the same position on every step.
  3. **Next is always available** (it is enabled before any action), on every step, whether or not the player has done what the step asks. **Skip tutorial** ends the tutorial straight away and starts the chosen match (from How to play it closes the tutorial and starts nothing). This replaces the earlier rule that Next waited for the step's action (U41 to U46, U136).
  4. **No text under the board.** The hint ("Tap a column.", "Locked in. You cannot change it now.") and the opponent's status line are gone. The practice board still shows what happened (discs dropping, the glowing column, the warning banner, the power-up button's own label).
  5. **Smaller than the marking viewports (below 360px wide or lower than 700px, such as 320 x 568 and 360 x 640).** The card is the screen's width below 360px, the board is 308px so each column stays 44px wide (docs/design.md), the instruction text is smaller (0.8rem) and the board sits just under its ghost lane instead of being centred. The top area is 160px (6 lines measured at both sizes). The card does not scroll. These are not marking viewports, so U167's fixed-size rules are measured only at the two marking viewports (Q41); the smaller screens are held to Q15 (no scrolling), Q16 and Q25.

## The card's height is the sum of its parts (added 2 November 2026, second fix list, ADR 0016 amended)

- **U172** *Replaces the card size and the "three fixed areas" grid of U167; everything else in U167 stays (one size per viewport, identical on all 11 steps, the board's size never changes, Next always on, no text under the board).* The 720px height was too tall and left large empty gaps around the board. The card is still **one fixed size per viewport and identical on every step**, but its height is now **calculated from its parts** instead of a large fixed number. Top to bottom, **every part is its own block in normal layout flow** (no absolute positioning, no negative margins, no grid rows with spare space), so nothing can overlap and no gap can be larger than the one written here:
  1. top padding, then
  2. the **instruction area** (step counter, title, instructions), still sized to the longest instructions at that viewport (U167: phone 204px, laptop 140px), then
  3. **16px**, then
  4. the **ghost disc lane**, a block one disc tall (`--ghost-size`, 34px) whose bottom edge the ghost disc sits on, then
  5. **8px**, then
  6. the **board** (its width is fixed per viewport, so its height is too), then
  7. **12px**, then
  8. **one reserved slot** as tall as the tallest thing any step puts there (the timer bar, the Arm shield or Use Earthquake button, the warning banner): 44px, then
  9. **24px**, then
  10. **Next** (44px), then
  11. **12px**, then
  12. **Skip tutorial** (44px), then
  13. bottom padding **equal to the top padding**.
  The card's height is the sum of those, so it is the same on every step. **Resulting card height** (padding as in U167: phone 10px top and bottom, laptop 24px): **phone 390 x 844: 746.5px** (10 + 204 + 16 + 34 + 8 + 274.5 + 12 + 44 + 24 + 44 + 12 + 44 + 10, plus the card's 5px border top and bottom), **laptop 1366 x 768: 699.3px** (24 + 140 + 16 + 28 + 8 + 269.3 + 12 + 44 + 24 + 44 + 12 + 44 + 24, plus the border; the ghost disc is 28px there). On a screen smaller than the marking viewports (U167 item 5) the gaps shrink to 4px and Next and Skip share one row so the card still fits one screen.
- **U173** *Nothing wider than the board; each step clears what it added.* (a) Everything in the slot under the board (the timer bar, Arm shield, Use Earthquake and the warning banner) is **centred and no wider than the board**, and the **timer bar is exactly the board's width**; its left and right edges are inside the board's. (b) **Each step clears what it added** when the player leaves it, by Next or by Skip tutorial (the tutorial has no Back button): banners, highlighted columns, power-up buttons, timer bars and demo discs are removed and the next step starts from its own clean state. So the warning banner shows only on step 8, Arm shield only on step 6, Use Earthquake only on step 7 and the timer bar only on step 4. (c) **Nothing overlaps**: no two visible items in the card (the step counter, title, instructions, ghost disc lane, board, everything in the slot, Next and Skip tutorial) overlap, and every item is fully inside the card's padding.
- **U175** *The instructions are centred in their area (added 2 November 2026; changes "content sits at the top of the area" in U167).* Inside the instruction area (U172 item 2) the step counter, the title and the instructions together are **centred vertically**, so a step with shorter instructions has **equal blank space above and below** its text instead of one gap underneath. The three stay together as one block (the title is not separated from its instructions). Nothing else changes: the area keeps the height of the longest instruction, the card size, the board's size and position and every instruction's words are as before.
