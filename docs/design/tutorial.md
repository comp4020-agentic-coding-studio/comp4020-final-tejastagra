# First-time tutorial

Rubble has rules a newcomer has never seen: both players move at once, matching columns crash into rubble, each player has one shield and one Earthquake, rounds are timed and the board shrinks over time. The tutorial teaches all of it, and how to play with others, in eleven short steps.

## Rules (to add to docs/rules.md)

- **R47** The tutorial is shown once, automatically, the first time a person starts a game in their browser (Play the computer, Play a friend, Join with a code or a friend's link), before that game begins (changed 31 October 2026; see docs/rules.md).
- **R48** Finishing or skipping the tutorial is saved with the player, so it does not return after a refresh, a new session, a restart or a redeploy.
- **R49** The tutorial can be skipped at any step and replayed at any time from "How to play".
- **R50** Tutorial games never change the leaderboard.

## Steps (rewritten 29 October 2026, Part 7 of the fix list; replaces U41 to U46)

The tutorial is an overlay over a practice board against a scripted computer. It is interactive wherever an action is involved: a step that asks for an action shows Next only once the action is done. A progress line (`tutorial-progress`) says **"Step N of 11"**. **Skip** (`tutorial-skip`) is always there. There is no disc left in the top hole.

- **U136** **When it shows (R47, changed 31 October 2026).** It opens only when a person is about to play **their first game**, over the screen they started from and before the game: on home when they tap Play the computer, Play a friend or join with a code (after the code popup accepts a code), and on a game link (`/g/CODE`) **only if the seat check says a player seat is free** (R100); the page asks first and waits for the answer. It does **not** open: for Quick match; on home or any other page by itself; for a spectator (a link to a game that is full, which goes straight to watching, `/watch`); for a game that has **ended** (the page goes straight to its result); or for a signed-in player whose account has finished a game or has seen it (R48). Whether it has been seen is kept in the browser's local storage (`rubble-tutorial-seen`) and on the account. The last step's main button reads **"Start the match"** (`tutorial-next`) and starts the game the person chose; Skip starts it too. From How to play the last button reads **"Done"** and Skip reads **"Close"**, and no game starts.

1. **U137 The goal.** "Get four of your discs in a row, across, down or diagonally." The practice board shows four red discs in a row glowing. No action: Next shows at once.
2. **U138 Pick a column.** "Tap a column to choose it. Your choice locks in and cannot be changed this round." The player taps a column; the ghost disc locks in.
3. **U139 You both move at once.** "Your opponent chooses at the same time. Neither of you sees the other's choice, and both discs drop together." The player taps a column; the computer chooses another and both drop.
4. **U140 The round timer.** "Each round lasts up to 10 seconds. The bar under the board shows the time left. If you do not choose in time, you miss the round. (Changed 1 November 2026, U162: the three-missed-rounds rule moved to step 11.)" A practice timer bar drains; the player taps a column before it runs out (it never runs out on its own).
5. **U141 Crashes.** "If you both pick the same column, the discs crash and leave rubble. Rubble blocks lines for both players." The player taps a column and the computer picks the same one: rubble.
6. **U142 Your shield.** "The computer is about to win in the glowing column. Arm your shield, then pick that column. The shield wins one crash, once per game, and is used up even if there is no crash." The player arms the shield and taps the glowing column; their disc lands and blocks the win.
7. **U143 Earthquake.** "Earthquake uses your turn: the bottom row crumbles and everything drops down one. Lines it makes by itself do not win. It works once per game, and only when the bottom row has a piece." The player uses it to break up the computer's line.
8. **U144 The shrinking board.** "After round 12, and again after round 20, the top row closes. A warning shows the round before. Plan ahead: space runs out." The warning shows, the player taps a column, and the top row turns to rock.
9. **U145 Draws.** "If you both complete a line in the same round, or the board fills up, it is a draw." The board shows both players completing a line at once. No action.
10. **U146 Playing with friends.** *Rewritten 31 October 2026.* "Play a friend gives you a link and a 6-letter code to send to anyone, anywhere in the world. The first person to open it plays you. If you send it to more people, the second person to open it, and anyone after them, can watch the game live as a spectator." A small illustration (`tutorial-friends`, drawn with shapes, no words) shows **you** (an avatar with a red disc), **the first friend joining as your opponent** (an avatar with a yellow disc, joined to you by a line) and **the second friend as a spectator** (an avatar with an eye icon, outside the pair). No action. The illustration sits in the practice board's place and takes no more room than the board (Q15).
11. **U147 Leaving and playing again.** "Leaving a game counts as a forfeit, so you lose. After a game you can play the same person again with Play again. If both players miss 3 rounds in a row, the game is abandoned. On a keyboard: keys 1 to 7 pick a column, S arms your shield, E uses Earthquake." No action. This is the only place the keys are written down (U18).

- **U47** Every caption is short, uses the words in `docs/glossary.md` and is readable on a 320px screen. Captions are announced in an `aria-live` region. The step's title is its name above ("The goal", "Pick a column", and so on).
- **U48** The tutorial works by tap, mouse and keyboard, and under reduced motion every step still makes sense without animation.

## Fitting one screen (added 31 October 2026, Q15 to Q17)

- **U148** *Order replaced by U159 on 31 October 2026.* The tutorial card never scrolls, at 390 x 844, 1366 x 768 and 320 x 568, on every step. The gap between the caption and the practice board is small (8px), the board is sized from the height left over (never below what keeps the columns tappable), and under the board come, with at least 16px between each, the step's action button (Arm shield or Use Earthquake, when the step has one), Next and Skip (on a short or narrow phone, Next and Skip share one row 8px apart, each at least 44px tall). Nothing, including a caption, status line or button, overlaps the board (Q16). If space is short the gaps shrink first, then the board, never a button below 44px tall.

## The tutorial's fixed order (added 31 October 2026, Part K of the second fix list)

- **U159** Top to bottom: the **step counter**, the **title**, the **caption**, the **opponent's status on its own centred line**, **16px**, **the ghost disc lane** (one disc's height, the practice board's top margin; there is no separate element), **8px**, the **practice board**, **16px**, the **hint text** (`tutorial-hint`), **16px**, the **step's buttons** (Arm shield or Use Earthquake where the step has one, then Next) **16px apart**, then **Skip**. *Tightened 31 October 2026: the hint sits 16px below the board, never against its edge, and no gap anywhere in this order is larger than 24px.* **No text, card, badge or button ever enters the ghost lane**, with a disc showing or not, so nothing moves when a disc appears. The caption is never overlapped by the ghost disc. If space is short the board shrinks, never the lane or the gaps (Q15 still holds at 320 x 568).

## Readable text and a fixed button place (added 1 November 2026, Fix 3 of the fourth list)

- **U161** The tutorial's text had shrunk too far to read on a phone and its buttons were off centre. **Minimum sizes, on every step at every viewport:** the step counter (`tutorial-progress`) **14px**, the title **24px**, the caption **17px**, the opponent's status (`tutorial-status`) and the hint (`tutorial-hint`) **16px**. If the card then runs out of room, **the practice board is made smaller, never the text** (the gaps and the ghost lane of U159 still hold). **Next** (`tutorial-next`) stands on its own, **centred horizontally** in the card, with **Skip** (`tutorial-skip`, a text link, never an outlined button) **centred below it**, in the **same place on every step**: a step that has no Next still keeps Next's place (hidden, never removed), so Skip stays exactly where it is. Next and Skip are never side by side. The warning banner on the shrinking board step (`tutorial-warning`) keeps **at least 16px from the card's edges** on every side, and nothing in the card overflows or touches the card's edge. The whole card still fits the screen with no scrolling (Q15). This replaces the shared row for Next and Skip in U148 and the smaller type in U159's compact variants. A browser check (Q32) measures all of it on every step. *Changed 1 November 2026 (U162): the pinned, fixed-place Skip and the card's fixed height are gone. Next and Skip now follow the content, and U162 sets their gaps.* **Two limits.** (1) The minimum sizes and the pinned buttons apply from **360px wide** up. At 320 x 568 the seven practice columns must stay at least 44px wide (308px), which leaves no height for text of this size, so the compact tutorial of U148 and U159 stays there and is recorded in `docs/handover.md` for Tejas. (2) Because Next and Skip are pinned to the same place, the gap between the hint (or the step's button) and Next is whatever height the card has left, so U159's "no gap larger than 24px" now covers only the gaps **above** the hint (status, lane, board, hint) and not the one above Next, from 360px wide up.

## The card's height comes only from its content (added 1 November 2026, Fix 1 of the fifth list)

- **U162** *Replaces the fixed card height and the pinned buttons of U161, and the fixed order and gaps of U159.* The tutorial card was fixed five times because each fix met one symptom. The cause was a card with a fixed height close to the screen's, with Next and Skip pinned to its bottom (`margin-top: auto`): when the content was shorter than the card, the leftover became a large empty gap. **The card's height now comes only from its content.** It has **no `height`, no `min-height`, no `height: 100%`, and nothing in it is pinned to the bottom** (no `margin-top: auto`, no `justify-content: space-between`, no `flex-grow`); no element reserves empty space (the hint has no `min-height` either). The card is **centred vertically on the screen**. Items stack in this order, with exactly these gaps: **step counter, 4px, title, 8px, caption, 8px, opponent status, 16px, the ghost disc lane (exactly one disc's height), 8px, the board, 12px, the timer bar (only on a step that shows it), 16px, the hint text, 24px, Next (centred), 12px, Skip (centred).** On a step with no timer the hint follows the board by 16px; a step's own button (shield, Earthquake) sits 12px under the hint. **If a step has no Next, Skip takes Next's place, with the same 24px above it.** The text sizes of U161 stay. If the card runs out of height, **only the practice board gets smaller** (its width is worked out from the height available, never below the 44px columns of U148), never the gaps or the text. **The timer bar is exactly the board's width**, its left and right edges aligned with the board's, as in the real game. The **step 4 caption** is "Each round lasts up to 10 seconds. The bar under the board shows the time left. If you do not choose in time, you miss the round."; the rule about three missed rounds is said only in the last step (U147). **Check (Q34):** on every step, for **every** pair of neighbouring visible items in the card (not a named list) the gap is at most 24px, the last item is at most 32px above the card's bottom edge, the card fits the screen without scrolling, and a shown timer bar's edges match the board's within 2px. **Movement (Q25 under U162):** because the card is centred and as tall as its content, Next appearing re-centres the whole card, so "nothing moves when the disc appears" is measured inside the card (the board's distance from the caption), not against the screen.
