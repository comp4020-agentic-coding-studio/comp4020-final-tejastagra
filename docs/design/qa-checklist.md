# QA checklist

Added on 28 October 2026. These are the layout and polish rules every screen must meet, each stated so it can be measured. `e2e/layout.spec.ts` checks the measurable ones on every screen at both viewports, `/visual-qa` (`.claude/commands/visual-qa.md`) checks all of them by looking at screenshots, and the design-reviewer and phone-tester use this list as their checklist. A screen is not finished until it passes every Q that applies. They sit on top of `docs/design.md` (viewports, tap targets, contrast) and `look-and-feel.md` (the toy style).

"Both viewports" means 390 x 844 (phone) and 1366 x 768 (laptop), and the checks also run at 320 x 568, the narrowest phone.

| ID | Rule | How it is measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Q1** | **No layout shift.** Nothing ever expands inline and pushes other elements. Editing a nickname, joining with a code, info explanations, sign up, sign in, change password and every confirmation open in a **centred popup dialog** over a dimmed page, with a close button and Escape to close. Opening or closing one moves nothing on the page behind it. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: the bounding box of every element behind a popup is the same, within 1px, before it opens and while it is open. Every popup has `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, a close button, closes on Escape and returns focus to what opened it. |
| **Q2** | **Button text never wraps.** Every button label fits on one line at both viewports. Shorten the label or widen the button instead. | A button's height is at most one line of its text plus its padding and outline. |
| **Q3** | **Boxes fit their content.** A card is only as wide as its content needs: at most 480px for forms and status messages and 560px for lists, centred. No wide boxes with short left-aligned text. | A card's width never exceeds its limit; a card holding only a short line or one control is not wider than its content plus padding. |
| **Q4** | **Consistent spacing.** At least 16px between stacked cards and buttons, and 32px between page sections. No two boxes touch. | The gap between the bottom of one card or button and the top of the next in the same column is at least 16px, and at least 32px between sections. |
| **Q5** | **Headings line up with their content.** Every page heading sits directly above its content and shares its left edge, inside the same centred column. | A heading's left edge is within 4px of the left edge of the content below it. |
| **Q6** | **One centred content column on every page.** | The page's main column is centred within 16px of the middle of the screen. |
| **Q7** | **Forms that edit an existing value are prefilled with it.** | The nickname field opens holding the current nickname. |
| **Q8** | **Controls only appear when they make sense.** Never "Sign out" for a guest, never sign-in or sign-up forms for a signed-in player. | A guest's page has no Sign out; a signed-in player's page has no sign-in or sign-up control. |
| **Q9** | **Every screen except home has a visible way to leave:** Home, Cancel or Leave game. | Every screen but `/` has a button or link named Home, Cancel, Cancel game or Leave game, on screen without scrolling at 320 x 568 where the screen fits, and reachable by tab. |
| **Q10** | **Empty states explain themselves.** An empty list says why it is empty and what to do next, in words; it is never blank or "Loading..." for ever. | Every list has text when empty. |
| **Q12** | **The way home is always visible.** Every screen except home has a **header bar** at the top (`topbar`) with a **Home** button (`header-home`) on its left, visible on load at both viewports without scrolling. No page relies on a Home button at the bottom. On the game screen Home asks to confirm leaving, because it is a forfeit (U107). | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: on every screen but `/`, `header-home` lies inside the viewport when the page loads, at 320 x 568, 390 x 844 and 1366 x 768, and is at least 44 by 44px. |
| **Q13** | **Visual centring.** The main thing on a screen (the board, a card or a list) is centred on the page, and nothing sits beside it in a way that pushes it off centre. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: the board's centre is within 16px of the page's centre on the game screen and the friend waiting screen, at every viewport; Q6 covers the column on every other screen. |
| **Q14** | **Players always look the same to everyone.** A player's colour and marker are the same on both players' screens and every spectator's screen. | `e2e/player-colours.spec.ts`: in one friend game the same seat's discs have the same colour and marker on both players' screens and a spectator's. |
| **Q15** | **No scrolling on game, tutorial and popup screens.** Everything, including every button, fits on one screen at 390 x 844, 1366 x 768 and 320 x 568. If space is short, reduce empty gaps first, then sizes, but never below the 44px tap target minimum (docs/design.md). | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: the page's `scrollHeight` is at most its `clientHeight` on the game screen, on every tutorial step and in every popup, at all three viewports. |
| **Q16** | **Nothing overlaps.** No button, caption or card ever overlaps the board or another element. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: no button or caption's bounding box intersects the board's on the game screen or in the tutorial. |
| **Q17** | **Even spacing in stacks.** In any vertical stack of cards and buttons, every gap is the same size. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: on the home screen the gaps between the player card and each menu button are equal within 2px, on a phone and a laptop. |
| **Q18** | **Popups are not cramped.** Inside every popup (the dialogs, the end popup, the waiting card and the tutorial card) there is at least **16px between the card's edge and its content**, at least **16px between side-by-side buttons** and at least **12px between a button's label (with its icon) and the button's edge**. If two buttons cannot sit side by side with that spacing at a viewport, they are **stacked full-width with 12px between them**, the main one first. A title's shadow sits cleanly behind its letters and does not look doubled. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: for every popup at 320 x 568, 390 x 844 and 1366 x 768, the content is at least 16px from the card's edges, buttons side by side are at least 16px apart and stacked ones at least 12px, and each button's label and icon are at least 12px from its edge. |
| **Q19** | **A result is shown once.** While the end popup is showing, the result line under the power-ups is hidden, so the result's words appear only in the popup. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: with the end popup open exactly one visible element holds the result's words. |
| **Q20** | **A name never takes more than two lines** in a player card on a phone. It is shrunk slightly if needed and cut with an ellipsis, and the tag (the `#` and four characters) stays visible. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: at 320 x 568 and 390 x 844 each name element is at most two lines tall and its tag is inside its card. |
| **Q21** | **The keyboard never covers a form.** On a phone, when the on-screen keyboard is open, the focused field and the form's buttons stay visible above it. | `e2e/layout.spec.ts`: each form popup is opened at 390 x 844 and 320 x 568, its first field is focused and the viewport is shrunk to 300px tall (an open keyboard); the focused field and the primary button are fully inside the visible area. |
| **Q22** | **A player's avatar carries their disc colour and marker** in a game and on the watching screen: a red or yellow face with a ring or cross badge in its corner, the same on both players' screens and the spectator's. There is no VS badge on a phone, the header's Round label is at least 16px from the right edge, and nothing in the header or the versus bar overlaps or wraps beyond two lines. | `e2e/player-colours.spec.ts` and `e2e/layout.spec.ts` at 320 x 568 and 390 x 844. |
| **Q11** | **No page looks plain.** Every page has a header with the Rubble title and a short subtitle, and uses the toy style of `look-and-feel.md` (icons, outlined cards, hard shadows), not bare text boxes. | Every page has the title element and a subtitle line; its cards use `--outline` and `--shadow` from the theme. |

## The header bar (Q12)

Every screen except home starts with one header bar (`topbar`). It holds, from left to right: the **Home** button (`header-home`, a `--blue` button with the home icon and the word "Home", at least 44px tall, one line), an optional **middle** line (`topbar-title`, for example "Playing with Pip #4F2A", cut with an ellipsis when too long) and an optional **right** item (`topbar-right`, for example "Round 3"). It is as wide as the page's content column (at most 560px on single-column pages, the board group's width on the game screen) and centred with it. The Rubble title and the page's subtitle (Q11) sit under it. Because it is the first thing on the page, it is always on the first screen on load.

Where it appears (U131, added 29 October 2026): Quick match (Home leaves the queue, as Cancel does), the leaderboard, the account page, About (`/readme/`), the watching screen and the game screen. On those pages the Home button at the bottom is removed. The game screen's bar (Part 3 of the fix list, `game-screen.md` U132) also carries "Playing with <opponent>" in the middle and the round on the right, and its Home opens the leave popup while a game is being played (U107); while waiting for the second player it cancels the game (U108), and after the game has ended it goes Home, withdrawing any rematch offer (R83). The watching screen's bar has Home only, and its "Watch another game" stays under the board. A popup's own Home (the end popup, the no-live card) stays: it is the answer to that popup.

## Popups (Q1)

One popup component serves every dialog (`src/client/dialog.ts`, styled in `public/components.css`): a `--panel` card at most 480px wide, centred over a `--scrim` dim, a heading, its content and a close button (an X at least 44 by 44px) and, where there is a choice, buttons in a row that each fit on one line (Q2). Opening a popup moves focus into it; Tab stays inside it; Escape and the close button close it and put focus back where it came from; the page behind does not scroll. The page behind it is never resized or moved, because a popup is `position: fixed` and takes no space. Under reduced motion it appears with no animation. The popups are listed in `docs/design/popups.md`.

## Checks that are measured and checks that are looked at

Q1 to Q9, Q12, Q13 and Q15 to Q22 are measured by `e2e/layout.spec.ts`, and Q14 by `e2e/player-colours.spec.ts`. Q10 and Q11 are judged by `/visual-qa`, which also looks at every other Q and at things a measurement cannot see (is anything cramped, does the page look finished, is any text cut off). `/visual-qa` writes its screenshots to `reports/visual-qa/` (not tracked by git) and fixes what it finds.
