# Quick match

Added on 28 October 2026 (Part 3 of the fix list). Quick match is a waiting room, so it has to look like a place a player is happy to wait in, and say what is going on. It follows `qa-checklist.md` (Q1 to Q11).

- **U96** `/play/quick` has the page header every page has (Q11): the cracked "RUBBLE" title and the subtitle "Quick match" in the heading style. Under it sits **one compact centred card** (`quick-panel`), at most 480px wide (Q3), and under that a **Cancel** button (`quick-cancel`) 16px below the card (Q4). Nothing else is on the page: one centred column (Q6).
- **U97** The card shows, from top to bottom: an **animated pair of discs** (`quick-discs`, a red ring disc and a yellow cross disc, so they differ by shape as well as colour, that swing towards each other and part again, using only `transform`, and standing still under reduced motion); the line **"Finding you a random opponent"** (`quick-status`, the heading style); the subtext **"Quick match pairs you with another person who is looking for a game right now"** (`quick-hint`, `--text-muted`); and the live count (`quick-live`).
- **U98** The live count reads **"N people playing now"**, where N is twice the number of live quick match games from `/api/live` (R80), **"1 person playing now"** is never shown (a game has two people), and with no live game the line is **"Be the first to play"**. It is asked for when the screen opens and every 10 seconds while it is open (R77), and keeps its last words if the server cannot say.
- **U99** After **20 seconds** of waiting the offer of the computer appears **inside the same card** (`computer-offer`): "Nobody has arrived yet. Play the computer while you wait?" with one button, **Play the computer** (`computer-offer-play`). The card grows downwards only; the Cancel button below it moves down with it and nothing above it moves (Q1). Waiting continues until the player taps it or cancels.
- **U100** A message from the server or a failure ("Could not join Quick match. Try again.", "You are no longer waiting. Go back and try again.", "Could not start a game against the computer. You are still waiting.") replaces the status line inside the card. Cancel leaves the queue and goes Home (Q9).
- **U101** The page works at 320px (the card's padding and the discs are smaller there, and the 16px between the card and Cancel stays, so Cancel is on the first screen even with the computer offer showing): the card is the screen width minus 32px, every line wraps within it, the discs are at least 44px apart from the edges and Cancel is on the first screen.
