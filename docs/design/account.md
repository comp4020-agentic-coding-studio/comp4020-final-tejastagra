# The account page

Added on 28 October 2026 (Part 6 of the fix list). It replaces the page of several forms with one card and popups, and follows rules R28 to R31 and R86 to R91 and `qa-checklist.md`.

- **U114** `/account` has the page header every page has (Q11): the cracked "RUBBLE" title and the subtitle "Your account". Under it is **one centred card** (`account-card`, at most 480px, Q3) and, below it, the history and stats (U118). Never more than one form is on the page at once: every form is a popup (`popups.md`), so nothing expands or moves (Q1). A **Home** button (`go-home`) is at the bottom (Q9), 32px under the last section (Q4).
- **U115** A **guest** sees in the card: their avatar, their nickname and tag, an **Edit** button (`edit-nickname`) that opens the nickname popup prefilled (Q7), two buttons, **"Save my progress"** (`open-signup`, opens the sign-up popup) and **"Sign in"** (`open-signin`, opens the sign-in popup), and the line **"Save your progress to keep your record on any device."** A guest never sees Sign out or Change password (Q8).
- **U116** A **signed-in player** sees: their avatar, their nickname, their username, a line **"Signed in"**, **Edit nickname** (`edit-nickname`, prefilled), **Change password** (`open-password`, opens the change password popup) and **Sign out** (`signout`). They never see the sign-up or sign-in buttons (Q8).
- **U117** Errors appear **inside the popup, directly under the field** they belong to (`popups.md` U88), never on the page behind. A confirmation ("Signed in.", "Password changed.") (the nickname has none, the new name is the confirmation, U32) closes the popup and shows once in the card, in a line that is always reserved, so nothing moves.
- **U118** Under the card, a **Your record** section (`history`) shows the stats and the match history of R90 and R91. The **stats** are two small cards side by side on a laptop and stacked on a phone, headed **"Against people"** and **"Against the computer"**: games played, wins, losses, draws and win rate (a whole percentage), or **"No games yet"** in place of the numbers when there are none. The **history** is a list of the last 20 games, newest first, each row an avatar, the opponent's name or "Computer", the result, "<n> rounds" and how long ago ("2 minutes ago", "3 days ago"). An abandoned game says "Abandoned" and is greyed. With no games the list says **"No games yet. Play the computer or a friend and they will show up here."** The section is the same width as the card (Q3, Q6), with 32px between it and the card (Q4).
- **U119** After a successful **sign-in**, the page and its history are reloaded so the guest history that moved into the account (R89) is shown at once.
- **U120** The sign-in popup (`signin-dialog`): username, password, a **"Forgot your password?"** link (`forgot-link`, at least 44px tall) and **Sign in** and **Cancel**. The link opens the forgot popup (`forgot-dialog`), which says **"Email game@tejastagra.com with your username and we'll reset it for you."** with one **Close** button and nothing sent. After 5 failed attempts for a username the popup shows "Too many attempts. Try again in a few minutes." under the password field (R88).
- **U121** The sign-up popup (`signup-dialog`): username and password (at least 8 characters, shown as a hint under the field), **Save my progress** and **Cancel**. The change password popup (`password-dialog`): current password, new password, **Change password** and **Cancel**.
- **U122** An account that signs in with a temporary password (R87) sees the **forced change popup** (`must-change-dialog`) at once: "Choose a new password" with one field and **Set password**. It has no close button, ignores Escape and a tap on the dim, and cannot be left until the password is set; a reload shows it again.
