# The leaderboard

Added on 28 October 2026 (Part 7 of the fix list). It follows rules R37 to R39 and R92 and `qa-checklist.md`.

- **U123** `/leaderboard` has the page header every page has (Q11): the cracked "RUBBLE" title and the subtitle "Leaderboard". Under it is **one centred column** (`leaderboard-column`, at most 560px, Q3 and Q6) holding, in order, the tabs, the podium and rows of the current tab, "Your best:" and a Home button. The headings, tabs, podium and rows all share the column's left and right edges (Q5).
- **U124** Two **tabs** (`tab-wins` "Most wins", `tab-rounds` "Fewest rounds"), each at least 44px tall with its label on one line (Q2), side by side and filling the column. Exactly one is selected (`aria-selected`), marked by a filled background as well as colour. Switching a tab shows its list and hides the other, and nothing else moves (Q1). The page opens on Most wins.
- **U125** The **top three** are shown on a **podium** (`podium`): first in the middle and tallest, second on the left and third on the right, each a block of different height with a number (1, 2, 3), the player's avatar and name above it and their score on it, in the toy style (outlines, hard shadows, `--yellow` for first, `--blue` and `--red` for second and third as bands). With fewer than three players the missing places are simply not shown, and with none there is no podium. A long name wraps inside its block and never widens the page.
- **U126** From the fourth place on, **compact rows** (`leaderboard-wins`, `leaderboard-rounds`, the same hooks as before) show the rank, the avatar, the name and the number at the right edge, 12px apart (Q4 allows it because they are one list), up to the 20 the server sends. The rows of the top three are the podium and are not repeated as rows.
- **U127** The **current player's row is highlighted** (`data-you="true"`: a `--yellow` outline and a "You" tag beside the name, so it differs by more than colour) wherever they appear, on the podium or in the rows. Under each list, **"Your best:"** (`your-best`) always shows: under Most wins "Your best: N wins" (0 wins if none), and under Fewest rounds "Your best: N rounds" or "Your best: no wins yet". A visitor with no player sees "Your best: play a game to get on the board".
- **U128** With **no entries** the list shows, in the column and instead of the podium, **"No wins yet. Only games against other people count, so play a friend or try Quick match."** with two buttons, **Play a friend** and **Quick match**, 16px apart. The same words are used for the Fewest rounds tab with "No wins by four in a row yet." in front. Only games against another person count (R37).
- **U129** A list that cannot be loaded shows "Could not load the leaderboard. Try again." in `--error`, with a **Try again** button, and the tabs and Home still work.
- **U130** *Changed 31 October 2026.* The home screen no longer has a Top players strip (home.md U35); this page is the only place the leaderboard is shown.

## Top 10 only (added 31 October 2026)

- **U131b** The page shows **at most 10 players** per tab. The **podium holds exactly the top 3** (or fewer when there are fewer players, U125) and the **list starts at place 4** and ends at place 10, so it never has more than 7 rows. The page asks the server for 10 (`/api/leaderboard?limit=10`). This replaces the "up to the 20" of U126.
